## Product Strategy (Expanded as Requested)

### Product Families

I will model **hierarchical products**, not flat names.

#### Product Line (Level 1)

- Tractors
    
- Tillers
    
- Reapers
    
- Harvest Equipment
    
- Attachments
    
- Implements
    

#### Sub-Categories (Level 2)

**Tractors**

- Compact Tractors
    
- Utility Tractors
    
- Row Crop Tractors
    
- Orchard Tractors
    

**Tillers**

- Mini Tillers
    
- Power Tillers

**Reapers**

- Self-Propelled Reapers
    
- Tractor-Mounted Reapers
    

**Harvest Equipment**

- Combine Harvesters
    
- Mini Harvesters
    

**Attachments**

- Loaders
    
- Backhoes
    
- Dozer Blades
    

**Implements**

- Ploughs
    
- Seeders
    
- Sprayers
    
- Cultivators
    

### Product Mix Enforcement

At the **sales invoice level**:

- Tractors ≈ **40%**
    
- Tillers ≈ **30%**
    
- Reapers ≈ **30%**
    
- Other products appear as:
    
    - Attachments bundled with tractors
        
    - Implements sold standalone at low ticket size
        

This creates:

- Cross-sell scenarios
    
- Multi-line invoices
    
- Better BI stories
